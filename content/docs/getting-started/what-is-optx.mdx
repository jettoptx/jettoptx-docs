## We Build Augments

> An Augment is a digital extension of yourself. Not a skill. Not an application.

That is the product. Skills help an Augment. Apps are surfaces. The buyer word is **Augment**.

## OPTX Auth - It's you

Auth should feel like recognition, not a lecture:

- **It's you**
- **Email, X, or wallet**

Integrators use [`@jettoptx/auth`](/docs/sdk/auth). Deep protocol pages may say JETT Auth - Start and Build lead with **OPTX Auth**.

Gaze vectors are processed on-device; only opaque proof hashes leave the device for attestation.
