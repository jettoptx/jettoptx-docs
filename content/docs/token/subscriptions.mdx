JettChat doesn't require a Solana wallet. Pay USD via Stripe and get full tier access.

## Subscription tiers — monthly billing

| Tier | Price |
|------|-------|
| **MOJO** | $8.88 / mo |
| **DOJO** | $28.88 / mo |
| **SPACE COWBOY** | $88.88 / mo |

See [Tiers](/docs/token/tiers) for the full feature breakdown of each.

## No JTX price peg

JTX has **no fixed dollar equivalence**. Subscription prices are set in USD via Stripe.
JTX trades freely on Solana DEXes — its market price is determined by supply and demand, not pegged to any tier price.

## Space Cowboy NFT — lifetime access

Instead of a monthly subscription, you can burn **1,111 JTX** to mint a **Space Cowboy NFT** for lifetime Space Cowboy tier access. The JTX is transferred to the protocol treasury permanently. Only 1,111 NFTs will ever exist.

## Cancellation

Subscriptions can be cancelled at any time; access continues through the end of the billing period.
Space Cowboy NFT holders retain access as long as the NFT is in their wallet — no expiry.
