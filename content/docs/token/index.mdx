
SPL token, 9 decimals. **Total supply: 4,400,000 JTX. Mint authority revoked.**

## No price peg

JTX has **no artificial price floor, ceiling, or dollar peg**. Its price floats freely on Solana DEXes (Jupiter, Raydium). Supply is fixed and deflationary — JTX is permanently removed from circulation whenever a Space Cowboy NFT is minted.

## What JTX does

- **Space Cowboy NFT minting** — Burn 1,111 JTX to mint one of 1,111 Space Cowboy NFTs (lifetime platform access + governance rights). JTX goes to treasury permanently.
- **Governance** — Space Cowboy NFT holders vote on protocol direction, feature priority, and treasury allocation.
- **Trading** — Freely tradeable on Solana DEXes.
